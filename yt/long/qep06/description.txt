Do traders, ek hi system, ek hi trades. System me edge hai, phir bhi saal ke aakhir me ek ka account aadha ho gaya. Farak sirf ek tha: har trade me kitna risk liya. Is episode me zero se samjho Risk of Ruin, yaani account doobne ka chance, aur use kaise chhota rakhein.

Ye SPIDER ki Quant Trading Series ka Episode 6 hai (Hindi + English subtitles).

Is episode me:
• Ruin kya hai: wo giraavat jahan se wapas aana lagbhag naamumkin (yahan: account aadha)
• Ruin teen cheezon pe tika hai: win rate, jeet ÷ haar (R), aur risk % per trade
• Haar ki lambi line kitni aam: 50% jeet, 500 trades → 8 lagataar haar ~62%, 10 lagataar ~21%
• 10 lagataar haar ke baad: 1% risk −10%, 2% −18%, 5% −40%, 10% −65%
• Simulation (50% jeet, jeet = 1.5 × haar, 200 trades, 20,000 baar): account aadha hua → 1–2% risk ~0%, 5% ~2%, 10% ~18%, 20% ~57%
• Fixed lot formula: Ruin ≈ (q ÷ p)^N — 55% jeet pe N=5 → 37%, N=25 → 1% se kam
• Ruin kaise ghatayein: chhota risk, loss limit, aur bina edge koi size safe nahi
• Apna ruin level pehle se tay karo
