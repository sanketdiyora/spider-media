Is episode me:
• Imbalance = ek taraf ka number doosri taraf se kai guna bada
• Tulna tirchhi kyun: Ask ko ek neeche wale Bid se, Bid ko ek upar wale Ask se milao
• Udaharan: 2653 ask 150 ÷ 2652 bid 30 = 5 guna (500%). Aam seema 300%, kuch log 400%
• Poori candle: 2651, 2652, 2653 pe lagataar buy imbalance = stacked imbalance
• Sell imbalance: 2659–2661 pe stacked sell (7, 4.2, 4.3 guna)
• Chhote numbers ka dhokha: 3 vs 1 bhi 300% hai, isliye badi taraf kam se kam ~50 contract
• Zone baad me: 2670 se girke 2652 pe ruka, phir 2668. Zone support bana
• Jab zone toote: price 2644 tak gaya. Stop hamesha zone ke us paar
• Spot forex / gold me asli bid-ask volume nahi; asli footprint futures pe
