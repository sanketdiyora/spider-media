Do candles bilkul ek jaisi hari: ek jaisa open, ek jaisa close. Ek me buyers ne zor se dhakela, dusri me bas sellers peeche hat gaye. Ye farq ek number batata hai: delta. Is episode me zero se samjho delta aur cumulative delta (CVD).

Ye SPIDER ki Order Flow Series ka Episode 13 hai (Hindi + English subtitles).

Is episode me:
• Delta = ask par volume − bid par volume (jaldi wale buyers − jaldi wale sellers)
• Ep 12 ki footprint candle: 655 − 390 = delta +265
• Plus, minus aur zero ke paas delta ka matlab
• Delta bars: har candle ka delta chart ke neeche
• CVD = delta ka chalta jod (example: 265 → 385 → 305 → 455 → 415 → 615)
• Price aur CVD saath naya high banayein = trend sehatmand
• Delta ≠ volume: volume 1000 par delta +20, volume 400 par delta +300
• CVD kab se ginein: har session/din zero se, ya anchored CVD
• Spot forex/gold ka sach: MT5 ka delta tick se andaaza hai; asli delta futures par
