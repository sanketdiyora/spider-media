Ek system 80% trades jeetta hai, dusra sirf 45%. Phir bhi paise me dusra saadhe teen guna zyada kamata hai. Is episode me zero se samjho system ka report card: profit factor aur Sharpe ratio, aur ye number kab dhokha dete hain.

Is episode me:
• Profit factor = kul kamaai ÷ kul nuksaan
• Example: 45 jeet × 2R vs 55 haar × 1R → PF 1.64, kul +35R
• Win rate ka dhokha: 80% jeet, par +0.5R jeet aur −1.5R haar → PF 1.33, sirf +10R
• PF kaise padhein: 1 se kam = ghaata; bahut trades par 1.3–2 aksar achha; 10 trades ka PF 3 kuch nahi batata
• Cost ka asar: har trade par 0.1R cost → PF 1.64 se 1.41
• Sharpe ratio = ausat return ÷ utaar-chadhaav (standard deviation)
• Example: A (2% ausat, 4% utaar-chadhaav) Sharpe 0.5 vs B (3%, 9%) Sharpe 0.33; saal ka ≈ × √12
• Sharpe ki seema: risk-free return, Sortino, aur martingale jaise system ke chhupe jhatke
• Poora report card: trades, PF (cost ke baad), ausat R, max drawdown + Monte Carlo, Sharpe
